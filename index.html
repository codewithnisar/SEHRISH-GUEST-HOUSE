EXPLORE GILGIT-BALTISTAN

Beyond Our Doors: Iconic Skardu.

Sehrish Guest House serves as your ideal launchpad to explore the world-famous natural wonders of Skardu. We arrange private 4x4 vehicles and experienced local guides.

4,114m Altitude
🌿 1.5 hrs from guest house ⭐ Must Visit

Deosai National Park

Known as the "Land of Giants", this endless alpine plateau is dotted with wildflower fields, crystal rivers, and the iconic Sheosar Lake.

35 Min Drive
⛵ Boating & Gardens 📸 Photography

Shangrila Lake Resort

A fairy-tale heart-shaped lake surrounded by Chinese-pagoda styled chalets, lush fruit orchards, and reflections of towering peaks.

15 Min Drive
🐪 Desert & Snow 🌅 Sunset Point

Katpana Cold Desert

The highest cold desert in the world, where white rolling sand dunes meet dramatic snow-capped Karakoram peaks. Magical sunsets.

40 Min Drive
🏊 Emerald Waters 🌲 Apricot Groves

Upper Kachura Lake

Pristine turquoise glacial lake surrounded by wild apricot trees and massive granite cliffs. Enjoy traditional wooden boat rides.

20 Min Drive
🎣 Trout Fishing 🏔️ Deep Blue Waters

Satpara Lake & Dam

A natural deep blue jewel supplying fresh glacial water to Skardu valley, enveloped by dramatic raw mountain sceneries.

Day Tour
🏰 400-Year Heritage 🌸 Shigar Valley

Shigar Fort & Heritage

Explore centuries-old wooden royal palaces, the serene Jarba Xo (Blind Lake), and traditional Balti craftsmanship.
